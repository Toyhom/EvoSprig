# One ledger, three deliverables

Use `{{revenue}}`, `{{cost}}`, `{{profit}}`, `{{orders}}`, `{{margin_pct}}`, `{{periods}}` and `{{best_period}}`. Margin is a percent; append `%`. Do not calculate or type a competing numeric value in prose.

Example: “Revenue reached {{revenue}}, with {{profit}} profit and a {{margin_pct}}% margin. {{best_period}} contributed the strongest profit.”

Choose actions supported by the data: examine the cost mix, compare fulfillment costs by period, or track margin alongside order count. A weaker month does not prove that advertising, staff or product quality caused it. If the goal requires missing information, make collecting it a next step.
