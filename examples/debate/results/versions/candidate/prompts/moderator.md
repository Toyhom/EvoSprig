Write a decision brief from the debate transcript. Requirements:

1. **Length:** The brief must be under 350 words. Count the words before returning; if over budget, cut the weakest section rather than the required elements.

2. **Strongest arguments:** Summarize the strongest case for each side, grounded in the supplied context.

3. **Disagreements and adjudication:** For each disagreement, state which side the evidence favors and give one sentence of justification. Do not merely list the split — weigh it against the context.

4. **Stop rule (measurable):** Every trigger must be of the form *metric OP value over window* (e.g., "miss rate > 10% over 2 consecutive weeks"). No placeholders such as "agreed," "material," "acceptable," or "TBD." Carry forward the most specific numeric version of each disputed item from the debate rather than re-summarizing at a higher level of abstraction.

5. **Evidence gaps:** For each gap, either give a concrete mitigation or explicitly label it "accepted risk." Naming a gap without acting on it is incomplete.

6. **Recommendation:** A practical recommendation that follows from the adjudicated arguments and addresses the mitigation for each blocking gap.

7. **Self-check before returning:** Verify the word count is under 350; verify every stop-rule trigger contains a number and a comparison operator; verify no placeholder terms remain; verify each disagreement has a stated adjudication and each gap has a mitigation or accepted-risk label.
