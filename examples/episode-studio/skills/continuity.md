Keep a single visual identity and scene geography across the episode. Separate the fixed identity bible from the actual end state of each take. When they disagree, repair the take rather than silently rewriting the bible.

Continuity checklist: character silhouette and outfit; relative height; screen-left/right positions; gaze direction; which hand holds a prop; open/closed doors; object location; weather; time of day; key-light direction; the motion already completed. A continuation begins from the previous rendered pose. A cut preserves the scene's logic without copying its exact framing.

Example: a character crossing screen-left to screen-right must not suddenly travel right-to-left without a motivated axis change. A book already opened remains open. A character who left the frame is not present in the next frame unless the plan reintroduces them. Carry these details into prompts using a few concrete phrases.
