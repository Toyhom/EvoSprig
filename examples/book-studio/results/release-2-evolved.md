**Chapter 1: Define the Event and Lock the Budget**

Our fictional event: a 30-person neighborhood park cleanup and picnic on Saturday, June 14, at Riverside Park, 9 a.m.–1 p.m. Purpose: tidy the park and build community.

Budget ($600): food and drinks $200; cleanup supplies (gloves, bags, grabbers) $120; tent rental $100; signage and first aid $60; thank-you supplies $40; contingency reserve $80.

Weather: book the park pavilion as indoor backup; cancel-or-proceed call by 7 a.m. Accessibility: confirm step-free paths, reserve two benches, and offer a seated sorting task.

**Checklist**
- [ ] Write one-sentence purpose statement
- [ ] Book venue and backup
- [ ] Allocate all $600 line items
- [ ] Confirm weather and accessibility plans

**Chapter 2: Recruit and Coordinate Volunteers**

For our 30-person park cleanup, recruit 8 volunteers via a free sign-up sheet. Assign roles: two greeters, four cleanup leads, one refreshments lead, one accessibility marshal. Brief everyone in a 15-minute huddle with printed task cards. Day-of communication uses a group text thread. Ensure step-free routes and seated tasks for volunteers with mobility needs. If rain triggers the indoor backup, shift greeters to the hall entrance and reassign cleanup leads to sorting stations. Keep all coordination free—no spend from the $600 budget.

**Checklist**
- ☐ Sign-up sheet shared and 8 volunteers confirmed
- ☐ Roles assigned and task cards printed
- ☐ Briefing huddle scheduled
- ☐ Group text thread created
- ☐ Accessibility needs of volunteers confirmed
- ☐ Rain-shift plan and contacts distributed

**Chapter 3: Run the Day and Follow Up**

Arrive 90 minutes early. Volunteers set up tables, signage, water station, and the step-free entry route. At the weather decision point (two hours out), check the forecast: fair skies mean the outdoor plan; rain or high wind triggers the indoor backup you booked in Chapter 1. Verify accessibility—clear paths, reserved seating, and a greeter at the entrance. Keep spending within the remaining $600 line items; hold a small reserve for ice or last-minute supplies. Teardown is a team task: pack, sweep, return borrowed gear, and confirm no one leaves alone after dark. Within 48 hours, send thank-yous and hold a 20-minute debrief.

**Checklist**
- [ ] Setup complete; signage and water station ready
- [ ] Weather call made; backup activated if needed
- [ ] Accessibility verified: step-free route, seating, greeter
- [ ] Safe teardown; gear returned
- [ ] Thank-yous sent; debrief held; budget reconciled

# Editorial Handoff: Volunteer Community Event Guide

## Introduction
This three-chapter guide walks a first-time organizer through planning a small volunteer event, using a consistent fictional scenario: a 30-person park cleanup and picnic at Riverside Park with a $600 budget. Each chapter closes with a practical checklist. The handoff below flags reading order, consistency fixes, and facts that need sourcing before publication.

## Reading Order
The chapters are correctly sequenced and should run as written: **Chapter 1 (Define and Budget) → Chapter 2 (Recruit and Coordinate) → Chapter 3 (Run the Day and Follow Up)**. Each builds on the last—Chapter 2's rain-shift plan depends on the backup venue booked in Chapter 1, and Chapter 3's weather call references that same backup. No reordering needed.

## Consistency Corrections
1. **Weather decision time conflicts.** Chapter 1 sets the cancel-or-proceed call at **7 a.m.**; Chapter 3 says the decision point is **"two hours out"** (which would be 7 a.m. for a 9 a.m. start—consistent—but the phrasing differs). Recommend standardizing to "7 a.m., two hours before the 9 a.m. start" in both chapters.
2. **Checklist bullet styles differ.** Chapter 1 uses `- [ ]`, Chapter 2 uses `- ☐`, Chapter 3 uses `- [ ]`. Standardize to one format (suggest `- [ ]`).
3. **Budget reconciliation wording.** Chapter 3 says "keep spending within the remaining $600 line items," but $600 is the *total*, not the remainder. Reword to "within the allocated line items, holding the $80 reserve."
4. **Volunteer count vs. attendee count.** Confirm the 8 volunteers are *among* the 30 attendees or *additional*—currently ambiguous. State it explicitly in Chapter 2.
5. **"Confirm no one leaves alone after dark"** is inconsistent with a 9 a.m.–1 p.m. event. Either remove or reframe as a general safety note.

## Facts Needing Sources
- **Tent rental at $100** and **supplies at $120**—cite a vendor quote or regional price range to justify figures.
- **Park pavilion as indoor backup**—verify Riverside Park (fictional) rules; if using a real park name, confirm pavilion reservation policy and any fees.
- **Accessibility claims** (step-free paths, reserved benches)—recommend citing ADA guidance or local parks accessibility standards.
- **"Free sign-up sheet"**—name a specific tool (e.g., a free form service) or note it's illustrative.
- **First aid allocation ($60)**—clarify whether this covers a kit, a certified first-aider, or both; sourcing strengthens credibility.

**Note:** Riverside Park and all specifics are fictional; add a brief disclaimer so readers don't mistake the scenario for a real venue.
