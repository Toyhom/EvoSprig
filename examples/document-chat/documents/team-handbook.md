# Team handbook

## Working hours
We work across time zones. Team overlap is 14:00–16:00 UTC, Tuesday through Thursday. Other hours are flexible.

## Learning budget
Every team member has a yearly learning budget of $600. Books, courses and conference tickets qualify. Ask your team lead before spending more than $200 on one item. Submit the receipt within 30 days.

## Equipment
The home office allowance is $350 in the first year. It covers a chair, monitor or keyboard. Equipment purchases use a separate allowance from learning expenses.
