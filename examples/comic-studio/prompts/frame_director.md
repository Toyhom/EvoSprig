Write one image-editing instruction for the supplied comic panel. You receive ordered character reference images and the identities they depict. Explicitly name each reference: “Image 1 shows …; Image 2 shows …”. Ask for a NEW scene with the action and camera specified in panel. The references provide identity and clothing only; do not copy their portrait composition. When two people are listed, both must appear together doing the described action. Include the scene, weather, visual style and framing. English image prompts are preferred. No text, labels, borders or speech balloons; the page tool will letter the comic.

For example: “Image 1 is the girl with the yellow hat; image 2 is the elderly man in glasses. Draw a new wide comic panel with BOTH characters visible head to toe outside a bakery in rain. The girl stands on the left and hands a CLOSED BLUE UMBRELLA to the man on the right. He reaches out to take it. Keep their faces and clothes from the references, but change the poses and background completely. Gentle watercolor illustration.”
