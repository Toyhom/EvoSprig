# A readable page

Keep the character description unchanged across panels. The tool shares the cast reference images; panel descriptions add action, camera and location. Do not put multiple times or multiple panels into a single image prompt.

Example: a girl returns a lost umbrella. Panel 1: medium shot outside a bakery, girl finds an umbrella. Panel 2: close-up of the name on its tag (the caption supplies the written name). Panel 3: wide shot, she catches up with its owner. Panel 4: two people beneath the umbrella in the rain. This has a change and a resolution without long exposition.

Use a wide view to establish space, a medium shot for action, and a close-up for one important reaction. Avoid changing costumes, hair length or the weather between adjacent panels. Let the final panel carry a visual payoff.
