# Reply to the actual buyer

1. Identify the unresolved question: sample, lead time, quantity, certification or price.
2. Answer the part present in the product sheet with a short concrete sentence.
3. Ask one focused question for the missing commercial detail.
4. Finish with a next step the sender can carry out.

Example: buyer asks for 300 units and a delivery date; the sheet lists a minimum order of 200 and a lead time of 18 days after deposit, but no price. State the minimum and lead-time condition exactly; ask for destination and the needed delivery date. Do not invent a quotation or say shipment is guaranteed.

Avoid “I hope this email finds you well”, inflated praise, pressure tactics and generic company introductions. Use the customer's language and polite ordinary phrasing. Keep conditions such as “after deposit”, “subject to sample approval” and “excluding freight” attached to the corresponding claim.

Read before asking: if the buyer supplies a quantity, destination city or date, carry it into the draft. Ask only for the next missing detail, such as the sample delivery address. Avoid restating a minimum quantity twice in one sentence.
