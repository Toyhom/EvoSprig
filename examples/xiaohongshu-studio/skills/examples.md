# Original writing examples

These examples teach structure. Their facts belong only to their stated inputs. User-supplied references can provide another voice; none authorize additional claims about the current source.

## tool — explain a tool through one decision

Source: A tool compares two drafts sentence by sentence and allows accepting individual edits. No usage statistics are supplied.

Title: 改完一篇稿，到底动了哪几句？

Body:
一篇稿改了三版，最难找的有时候是：第二版那句好开头去哪了。

这个对照工具会把两版文字并排放，改动落在哪一句，可以直接看到。想保留原来的开头、只接受后面的修改，也能逐条选。

适合经常来回改稿的人。下一次交稿前，把原稿和终稿放进去对一下，会比只盯着终稿清楚。

Why: a concrete task opens the post; one capability is explained without an invented trial or performance claim.

## event — put the reader's choice first

Source: A bookshop holds a repair workshop Saturday 14:00–16:00, 39 yuan including materials, 12 places, booking required, bring one damaged book.

Title: 那本舍不得扔的旧书，周六带来修

Body:
书脊松了、封面掉了，先别急着换一本📚

这周六书店有一场旧书修补，带一本想修的书来，现场练习基础修补。

时间：14:00—16:00
费用：39元，含材料
名额：12人，需要提前预约

出门前记得把那本书装进包里。

Why: readable logistics, exact conditions and one natural emoji; no invented address or promise to restore every book.

## practical — turn advice into a usable check

Source: Before a handover, record the file location, current status, next action and person responsible. No product or outcome measurement.

Title: 交接文档，先写清这四件事

Body:
接手一个任务，最怕打开文档只有一句“已推进”。

交接前可以过一遍：
① 文件在哪，给到能打开的位置。
② 做到哪一步，把还没做的也写上。
③ 下一步具体做什么。
④ 遇到问题找谁。

“方案已整理”可以改成“方案在共享盘A目录，预算还没核对，下一步找财务确认”。接手的人才知道从哪开始。

Why: a task-shaped opening and an explicitly hypothetical illustration, with no invented author experience.

## personal — keep a small real experience small

Source: On Sunday the author made two bowls of tomato-and-egg noodles, one with spring onion and one without. The author preferred the bowl without onion and wants to try a soft-boiled egg next time.

Title: 番茄鸡蛋面，试了两种放葱的办法

Body:
周日煮了两份番茄鸡蛋面，一份放葱，一份没放。

吃完更喜欢不放葱的那碗。

下次想换成半熟蛋试试。

Why: the actions, taste preference and future plan are all supplied. There is no invented family reaction, recipe recommendation or explanation of why one bowl tasted better. A short source can produce a short post.
